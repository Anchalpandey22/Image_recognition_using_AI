About VisionAI

An end-to-end image recognition platform, built as a complete AI + web engineering project.

How it works

VisionAI uses a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) to classify images. An uploaded photo is resized, normalized, and passed through trained convolution and pooling layers that progressively learn shapes, textures, and patterns. The final layer produces a probability distribution across all known classes, and the top five most likely matches are returned with their confidence scores.

By default, the system runs on a MobileNetV2 model pretrained on ImageNet (1,000 everyday object classes), so predictions work immediately without any training. If you train train_model.py on your own labelled dataset, the app automatically switches to your custom model.

Architecture
  • Input → Conv2D + MaxPooling (×3 blocks)
  • BatchNormalization + Dropout for regularization
  • Flatten → Dense(512) → Dense(256)
  • Softmax output layer over all classes
  • Adam optimizer with EarlyStopping, ModelCheckpoint, ReduceLROnPlateau
